The Current Challenge

The manual admission process
costs more than it seems.

Three structural failures turn every new hire into a friction point across HR, IT and the candidate experience.

01

Data copied manually across systems

Physical forms, emails and duplication across Gupy, RM and ServiceNow. Errors and delays in every single hire.

02

No traceability, no governance

Approvals and internal transfers with no system record. No manager knows where each candidate stands in the funnel.

03

Bureaucratic onboarding, zero digital experience

Occupational health exams by phone, physical documents, paper signatures. No unified portal for candidates or managers.

03
AI Differentiator

What AI does that
automation alone cannot solve.

Four capabilities that move admission from rule-based RPA into autonomous, context-aware workflows.

AI Document Extractor

AI reads, interprets and validates IDs and documents submitted by candidates. Automation just moves files, AI understands the content.

Autonomous Validation and Smart Blocking

AI detects document inconsistencies and blocks workflows automatically. No static rules: it learns from the history of past admissions.

Semantic Integration: Gupy + ServiceNow + RM

Connects all three systems with intelligent field mapping, not just sync. Eliminates copy-paste that no RPA fully solves end-to-end.

AI-Guided Onboarding and Digital Signature

Candidates receive personalized guidance in the portal, with next steps generated dynamically. Contract signed electronically in the same workflow.
