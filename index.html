Process Detail

Four flagships, four playbooks

A focused look at the four highest-impact processes: objectives, deliverables, current execution time and strategic notes.

Process 01

Contract Issuance

Automate eight contract issuance flows, from form submission to automatic delivery of signed contracts and payment slips to all parties.

Deliverables
  • Standardized offer in ServiceNow with integrated checklist.
  • Automated delinquency check (SERASA / ERP integration).
  • Automatic approval triggers based on a risk matrix.
  • Automatic delivery of contracts and payment slips post-signature.
  • Automatic notifications at every step of the flow.
732h/month → up to 90% reduction

Automation eliminates manual CRM data entry and ad-hoc payment slip issuance, centralizing control and communication in ServiceNow.

Process 02

Document Request

Enable self-service via chatbot or knowledge base, reducing the need to open tickets for simple document requests.

Deliverables
  • Self-service via chatbot and knowledge base.
  • Structured offer for Backoffice to act when needed.
  • Direct document delivery to client and/or seller via ServiceNow.
  • Centralized and standardized request management.
288h/month → up to 90% reduction

Eliminates manual spreadsheet tracking and ad-hoc lookups in external systems (Google, Notary Offices, Nexxera, Power Apps).

Process 03

Proposal Issuance

Standardize and automate proposal issuance, eliminating Word checklists and manual Business team engagement for pricing.

Deliverables
  • Standardized form with mandatory fields and automatic validations.
  • Automatic Business team engagement for pricing.
  • Automatic proposal generation and delivery via CRM.
  • Visibility and governance in a single system.
168h/month → up to 85% reduction

The "Non-registered Sales Item" flow is also addressed: Business creates the item without interrupting the main process.

Process 04

Contract Cancellation

Automate cancellation flows (Standard and Sesi Card) with automatic CRM status check and ERP / Signature Portal integration.

Deliverables
  • Automatic contract status check in CRM.
  • Distinct automated actions based on contract status.
  • Automatic generation of Termination Agreement (Sesi Card).
  • Automatic Finance team engagement for reversals.
  • Automatic notifications at every step.
18h/month → + Sesi Card volume included

Eliminates manual tracking of Finance tickets: all steps are tracked and triggered automatically in ServiceNow.
