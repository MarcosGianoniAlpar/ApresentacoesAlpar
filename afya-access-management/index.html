The Problem We Solved

The chaos in access management
was costing too much.

Three structural failures turned every grant, revocation and review into operational risk, audit debt and lost time across IT.

01

Manual and fragmented processes

Requests scattered across multiple channels, with no traceability and high operational risk.

02

High error rate and rework

Duplicate or incorrect requests consumed the IT team and generated continuous reprocessing.

03

Zero visibility over active access rights

Without centralized auditing, every grant or revocation required direct human intervention.

03
How We Solved It

Smart forms + end-to-end
automation.

Full refactoring of the Access Management module in ServiceNow, with modular architecture and a guided user journey from request to resolution.

Modular Architecture

Separation of requests by type (grant, revocation, maintenance) with independent, scalable workflows for each motion.

Automated Approvals

Automatic routing eliminates analyst intervention on standard cases: 85% of access grants flow through with zero human touch.

Virtual Agent via NIC

Virtual assistant cuts average wait time to 41 seconds and lifts satisfaction NPS to 4,42 across the support journey.
